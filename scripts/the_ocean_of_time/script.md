# The Ocean of Time
*A Short Screenplay*

Original screenplay as provided, kept here as the source of truth the
scene files in `scenes/the_ocean_of_time/` were broken down from.

## Characters

- **ANANYA (20s)** — Gentle, idealistic, and deeply loyal.
- **ROHIT (20s)** — Calculating, charming on the surface, ruthless underneath.
- **MR. SHARMA (50s)** — Orthodox, tradition-bound, rigid head of the family.
- **MRS. SHARMA (50s)** — Bound by societal reputation and orthodox values.
- **DOCTOR (40s)** — Clinical, tasked with delivering grave news.
- **PRIYA (20s)** — Rohit's secret girlfriend.

---

**EXT. SHARMA ESTATE - DAY**

An imposing, traditional mansion surrounded by high stone walls. The sun beats down with heavy, unyielding light.

**INT. SHARMA ESTATE - LIVING ROOM - DAY**

ANANYA (22) stands before her parents, MR. and MRS. SHARMA. They sit rigidly on antique mahogany chairs.

> **MR. SHARMA** *(Firm)*
> The family has prestige to uphold, Ananya. We have chosen a suitable match.

> **ANANYA** *(Softly, but resolute)*
> Father... I have already given my heart to someone.

Mrs. Sharma gasps softly. Mr. Sharma's eyes narrow.

> **ANANYA (CONT'D)**
> His name is Rohit. We studied together. He is honest and kind. Please... meet him once.

Silence fills the room. Mr. Sharma looks into his daughter's pleading eyes, his rigid posture slowly easing into reluctant acceptance.

**INT. WEDDING MANDAP - NIGHT**

Traditional decorations. Flashing camera lights.

ROHIT stands in wedding attire, smiling warmly for the guests. Ananya sits beside him, radiantly happy.

Behind Rohit's smiling facade, his eyes subtly glance around at the luxurious estate and jewel-encrusted decor, taking mental inventory.

**INT. ROHIT & ANANYA'S APARTMENT - NIGHT**

Months later.

Ananya sits alone at a dimly lit dining table. A cooked meal sits cold. She stares at the clock on the wall ticking past 2:00 AM.

The front door unlocks. Rohit walks in, adjusting his jacket. He glances at Ananya with utter indifference, skipping any greeting.

> **ANANYA** *(Quietly)*
> You didn't answer my calls again.

> **ROHIT**
> I was busy. Don't crowd me.

He walks straight to the bedroom, leaving her in silence.

**EXT. DOWNTOWN CAFE - DAY**

Rohit sits at an outdoor table, laughing warmly as he holds hands with PRIYA.

Ananya stands across the street, holding a small paper bag from a pharmacy. Her eyes fill with tears as she watches Rohit kiss Priya on the cheek.

She steps back into an alleyway, pulling out a positive pregnancy test from her bag. She stares at it, her chest heaving with silent sobs.

**INT. CLINIC - OPERATING ROOM - DAY**

Bright, sterile lights. Ananya lies on an operating table, looking terrified as the DOCTOR prepares the procedure.

> **DOCTOR**
> Are you certain about this, Mrs. Sharma?

> **ANANYA** *(Voice shaking)*
> Yes. Please... just get it over with.

**INT. CLINIC - RECOVERY ROOM - LATER**

Ananya wakes up. The Doctor stands beside her bed, his face pale and grave.

> **DOCTOR**
> There was a severe surgical complication during the procedure. Your uterine wall ruptured.

Ananya stares at him, paralyzed.

> **DOCTOR (CONT'D)**
> To save your life... we had no choice but to perform an emergency hysterectomy. Your uterus was removed. I am so sorry.

A tear falls silently down Ananya's cheek.

**INT. SHARMA ESTATE - STUDY - DAY**

Mr. Sharma slams a document onto his desk. Mrs. Sharma turns her back in disgrace.

> **MR. SHARMA** *(Trembling with rage)*
> An abortion? A secret procedure that leaves you sterile? You brought utter dishonor upon this family name!

> **ANANYA** *(Crying, kneeling)*
> Father, please! He was cheating on me! I had no choice—

> **MR. SHARMA**
> You chose him over us! Now you have ruined our lineage and reputation. You are no longer a daughter of this house. Leave!

**INT. ROHIT'S APARTMENT - NIGHT**

Ananya sits huddled on the floor of the dark living room, her small suitcase beside her.

Rohit enters, slamming the door. His face is twisted in fury.

> **ROHIT**
> Your father called me. He disowned you completely! Not a single cent of their wealth is coming to you!

> **ANANYA** *(Broken)*
> Rohit... I know about Priya. I know you never loved me.

> **ROHIT** *(Laughs harshly)*
> Loved you? You think I spent a single second caring about you? You were my ticket out of poverty! And now? You're useless to me. No inheritance... and you can't even bear an heir!

> **ANANYA** *(Defiant through tears)*
> You monster... I'm leaving. Everyone will know what you did—

> **ROHIT**
> No one is going to know anything.

Rohit grabs a heavy wooden decorative club resting on a stand nearby. Ananya's eyes widen in horror.

> **ANANYA**
> Rohit, no—!

Rohit swings the wooden weapon down with brutal force.

**FADE TO BLACK.**

**EXT. BARREN COASTLINE - DAWN**

The sky is a pale, melancholic gray. A fresh, unmarked mound of dirt rests near the shore, completely isolated.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She rests now in her quiet tomb, submerged beneath an endless ocean called Time. The world moved on, and all forgot her name.

**EXT. SKY - CONTINUOUS**

The blazing SUN rises high over the horizon, casting its harsh light over the ocean and the lone grave.

> **NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Only the Sun God above remains her eternal witness.

**FADE OUT.**
